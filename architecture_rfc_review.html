🎬 Desktop Master Workstation vs. Fireside Armchair Booth

Section 3 of RFC-001

The fundamental architectural decision of RFC-001 is that Desktop Studio defines the capability ceiling (multi-track, multi-take, dense timeline scrubbing), while Fireside Mobile distills this engine into an intimate, high-legibility, single-tap experience for elder narrators.

DESKTOP MASTER WORKSTATION /studio/production/[id]

Lean-Forward Director Workstation

  • Act I Script: Full Scriptorium with typewriter font (Courier Prime), section looping, beat reordering, and precision prompter customizer.
  • Act II Weave: Multi-column beat inspector with granular sensory cue overrides and word-density scoring.
  • Act III Capture: Multi-take folder (Take 1, Take 2, Take 3), granular WPM slider (80–180), external audio input selector (DJI Mic 2 vs USB), real-time VU meter, 16:9 widescreen video framing.
  • Act IV Review: Master Screening Suite with multi-track timeline scrubber, head/tail trim handles, side-by-side take comparator, and B-roll archival photo placement markers.
FIRESIDE ARMCHAIR BOOTH /studio/fireside

Lean-Back Living Room Companion

  • Act I Script: Armchair Script Editor with warm serif typography (Georgia / Merriweather), persistent Inspiration Spark card (💡), and tactile Sensory Seed chips (🌿).
  • Act II Weave: Single-column woven preview card with auto-illuminating modality pills and instant bilingual translation.
  • Act III Capture: One-tap Big Red Button ([ Start Talking ]), fixed elder prompter cadence at 120 WPM, portrait (9:16 / 4:5) mobile framing, zero technical dials, single [ ↺ Retake ], automatic ambient noise filter.
  • Act IV Review: Clean living room playback player, vintage physical album print scanner drawer, spoken postscript Bonus Memory drawer, 1-tap [ Seal & Complete Scene ], and direct Cinema Room TV casting bridge.
🔒 The Shared Invariant (Rule 48)

Both surfaces share the identical 31-scene curriculum (masterStoryStructure.ts), the identical NLP sensory anchor engine (detectSensoryAnchors), the identical Cloud Storage upload pipeline with CRC32c checksum verification, and identical encrypted Firestore metadata schemas.
